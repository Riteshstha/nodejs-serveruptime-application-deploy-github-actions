cat about.txt

I'm a networking and systems student focused on hands-on infrastructure labs, including routing, NAT, firewalls, and network monitoring. I learn by building real environments, then deliberately breaking them to understand how and why they fail.

My current lab runs on Proxmox and includes a pfSense firewall, segmented private application servers, an Nginx load balancer in Docker, and a monitoring stack built on Zabbix, Grafana, and Checkmk with email alerting. I'm now learning shell scripting and CI/CD to make these environments repeatable and automated.

Outside of tech, I play guitars and build home recording setups.
