I'm a networking student working through hands-on labs: routing, NAT, firewalls and network monitoring. I learn by building the real thing, then breaking it on purpose to see what fails.

My current lab runs on Proxmox: a pfSense firewall, private app servers, an Nginx load balancer in Docker, and Zabbix watching it all. I'm learning shell scripting and CI/CD to make it repeatable.

Away from the keyboard, I play electric guitar and build home recording setups.
